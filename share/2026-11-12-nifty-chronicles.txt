A tug-of-war on Dalal Street. 🪢

The Nifty dipped to 25,143 at the open, climbed to 25,385 as banks and finance companies bought, slid back to flat by 3 pm, and closed at 25,299, up 44 points. Bank Nifty rose 726 points; autos, FMCG and oil-and-gas pulled the other way.

Then, half an hour after the bell, October retail inflation came in just above 6%: the highest in two years and above the RBI's comfort band, with food prices doing most of the damage.
