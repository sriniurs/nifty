The fine print

What this edition does—and does not—claim

  1. Friday is the price cutoff. This single-day recap looks from 6 Nov toward Muhurat on 8 Nov. Prepared after the session, without incorporating Sunday’s prices or outcome.
  2. Friday-evening oil settlement and post-close institutional flow reporting are separate from the cash-market closing timestamp.
  3. Indicators are the dated provider snapshots, not a fresh independent calculation. No Sunday readings are silently mixed in.
  4. Daily comparisons are labelled daily; ten-session history and weekly macro context are explicitly marked.
  5. Rounded next-session pivots are mechanical references, not guaranteed support/resistance. Refresh them after Muhurat.
  6. No verified GIFT Nifty, Market Mood Index, fresh options dataset, scenario odds or intraday tape is invented.
  7. The short-session interpretations are editorial. Session length alone does not establish trend strength.
