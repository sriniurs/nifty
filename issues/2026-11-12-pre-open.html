Our call for today

OPINION

A careful day inside Wednesday’s walls

The Captain spends the day between 25,095 and 25,424, Wednesday’s low and high. Wall Street’s bounce and foreign buying hold the floor; dearer oil and the 4 pm inflation number keep a lid on the ceiling. He ends the day close to his balance point near 25,258.

Why
Overnight news pulled both ways: US chips up, oil up, rate-hike bets up. With India’s CPI due half an hour after the close, few traders will want a big bet before it. After an eight-session sprint, a pause is the natural move.
What pushes against it
Foreign investors bought over ₹12,500 crore on Wednesday alone. Another wave like that could punch through the ceiling.
What changed since last evening
Little. The Post Market verdict was “a breather is more likely than a sprint”. Wall Street’s rally softens that a little; oil’s bounce firms it up.
We are wrong if
The Nifty closes above 25,424 (a breakout) or below 25,095 (a slide). Either would mean the market didn’t wait for the inflation number.
