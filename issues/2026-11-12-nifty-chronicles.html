The 4 pm storm cloud

Inflation crossed the red line FACT

6%+

India’s retail inflation for October came in just above 6%, up from about 5½% in September. That is the highest in two years and the first time since October 2024 that it has crossed the top of the RBI’s 2–6% comfort band. It was also a touch hotter than economists expected.

Food did most of the damage: food inflation was close to 9%, and onion prices were more than double a year ago.

Released at 4 pm, after the close. [6] [7]

Why the Captain cares OPINION

The RBI already raised its policy rate by a quarter point in October and hinted more could follow. Its next meeting is on 2–4 December. A number above 6% makes another rise easier to argue for, and dearer loans are not what a rally wants to hear.

Prof. Earnings’ word of the day

OVERBOUGHT

A market is called “overbought” when prices have risen so far, so fast, that the momentum score (RSI) is above 70. It doesn’t mean prices must fall. It means the easy part of the climb is probably done, and even small bad news can cause a stumble. Today the Captain is overbought, and a hot inflation number is exactly that kind of news.

Our call for Friday

OPINION

A soft start, then the floor holds

The Captain opens weaker as traders react to the inflation number, and tests the first floor near 25,167. Bank buying and foreign money defend Thursday’s low of 25,143, and he ends the day between 25,143 and 25,385, Thursday’s low and high.

Why
The number was above 6% and a touch hotter than expected, so a first-hour wobble is the natural reaction. But foreign investors have been buying on almost every dip this month, and the banks that carry the Nifty were the strongest group today.
What pushes against it
If traders start pricing in a December rate rise, the banks could stop pulling, and the Captain is already overbought. A bad night on Wall Street would add weight.
Written before the overnight news
This call is made on Thursday evening. Check GIFT Nifty before 9 am: if it is far below 25,143, the morning may already be outside our walls.
We are wrong if
The Nifty closes below 25,143 (the floor gave way) or above 25,385 (the market shrugged off the number).
