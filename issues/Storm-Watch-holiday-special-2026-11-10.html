Stocks flinched, then steadied. The Nasdaq fell as much as 1.2% after the open but was back near flat by 8:31 pm IST; the S&P 500 was down 0.1% and the Dow 0.3%. [23] By about 9:05 pm, quotes showed the S&P 500 down about 0.4%. FACT · intraday, not closing

Oil kept sliding; gold slipped. Brent traded near $90, down about 2.5%. Gold fell about 2% to near $4,053. [23] [26]

The explainer

How a US price tag reaches Dalal Street

Hot US inflation↓Bigger or surer Fed rate hike↓Higher US yields, firmer dollar↓Emerging markets look less attractive; rupee under pressure↓Foreign buying in Nifty heavyweights can slow

Each link can weaken. Cheap oil pulls the other way: it eases India’s inflation and can cool future US price readings. Monday’s foreign buying of ₹7,212 crore is the flow to watch. OPINION

OPINION Our call for Wednesday

A higher open that runs into the wall

Why up at the open: Brent is near $90, about $8 below Friday’s settlement, which helps India more than most markets. US stocks absorbed the hot CPI within an hour instead of selling off. A December hike was already expected; the CPI mainly raised the odds of a bigger one. [14] [23] [25]

Why it stalls: the part that travels is the bond move. A two-year yield up about 19 basis points and a firmer dollar make foreign buyers, Monday’s ₹7,212 crore lifters, more cautious. Momentum is already stretched (RSI near 78) and Monday’s gain rested on banks. Expect the opening gain to be smaller than GIFT Nifty’s pre-CPI 1.2%, and selling around 25,084–25,140. [5] [8] [12]

We are wrong if: Nifty slips below 24,894 early (the bond storm reached Mumbai), or closes above 25,234 with broad buying (cheap oil won outright).
