Deep Dive · Why the Captain broke a sweat

The thermometer cracks 6 FACT

01 · India’s inflation

Retail inflation rose to just over 6% in October from about 5.5% in September. That is above the RBI’s 6% upper limit for the first time in two years. Food inflation jumped to nearly 9%. Economists now expect a rate hike when the RBI meets on 2–4 December. [10] [11]

The rate-sensitive groups took the hit: autos, realty and lenders. That is the market pricing costlier loans. OPINION

When the thermometer breaks the limit, the doctor reaches for the rate-hike medicine.

Our call for Monday

OPINION One probable path

A dip to test the 25,000 net, then buyers step in

Why lower first: inflation above the RBI ceiling makes a December hike likely, oil is back near $96, and momentum is stretched to an extreme. Friday set a lower high and a lower low, and 38 of 50 stocks fell. The easy path is a slip toward 25,050–25,000.

Why it holds there: foreign institutions have bought for nine straight sessions, VIX is falling rather than spiking, and the Nifty closed 90 points off its low. That zone also stacks Friday’s low, S1 and the round number. Expect dip-buyers there rather than a collapse.

We are wrong if: the Nifty closes above 25,246 (Friday’s high), which would make the dip a one-day blip, or closes below 24,956 (S2), which would mean a deeper cooling-off has begun.

One editorial view based on Friday’s data. Not a forecast, a trading signal or advice. Weekend oil and US data can change the opening.
