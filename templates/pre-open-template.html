Our call for today

OPINION

Gap-up, then a tug-of-war above the ceiling

The Captain opens well above 25,140 on cheaper oil and foreign buying. Hot US inflation and stretched momentum then cap the climb. He finishes the day above 25,140, but below his morning best.

Why
GIFT Nifty is up about 280 points, oil is down about 7% in two days, and foreigners have been heavy buyers.
What pushes against it
US rate-hike fears, momentum already “hot”, and India’s CPI tomorrow.
We are wrong if
The Nifty closes below 25,140. That would mean the gap failed and the bridge from Monday is back in play.
