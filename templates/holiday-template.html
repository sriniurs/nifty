Why the market is shut

NSE and BSE are closed for Diwali Balipratipada, called Bali Padyami in Karnataka. The day celebrates the legend of King Bali’s yearly return to visit his people, with lamps, rangoli and family feasts. Trading resumes on Wednesday 11 Nov at 9:15 am. [1]
