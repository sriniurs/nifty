The day in four frames

9:15 am

BOING!

The Captain jumped straight to 25,338, almost 300 points above Monday. Cheaper oil and returning foreign money did the pushing.

Heroes and laggards

Who carried the Captain FACT

  • Banks led the day, with PSU banks the strongest sector at about 3%.
  • Mahindra & Mahindra, IndiGo and Trent were the top Nifty gainers at the close.
  • Smaller companies joined in: the Smallcap 100 rose about 2%.

Source: closing report. [1]

Who sat it out FACT

  • IT lagged again. A firmer rupee is a headwind for dollar-earning software exporters.
  • Healthcare and pharma were the other weak spots.

Verdict!

Strong legs, hot head. After a run like this, a breather is more likely than a sprint. OPINION

The money trail

Foreign money is back FACT

Foreign institutions bought a net ₹7,212 crore of Indian shares on Monday, while domestic funds sold ₹7,173 crore. In the first week of November foreigners bought about ₹45,960 crore. Today’s figures come out later this evening. [9]

Cheaper oil, stronger rupee FACT

Brent crude sat near $92 a barrel, down about 7% in two days. The rupee strengthened to one of its best levels in months. India buys most of its oil abroad, so both help the import bill. [10] [11]

Prof. Earnings’ word of the day

GAP-UP

When the market opens well above the previous close, with no trading in between, the chart shows an empty “gap”. Today’s gap was almost 300 points, because a holiday gave good news two days to pile up. Gaps often get partly “filled” later on. Today the Captain gave back part of his jump by the close.
